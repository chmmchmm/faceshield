Privacy Policy

Face Shield: Kids Face Blur · iOS · Effective 6 October 2026

Summary: Face Shield collects no personal data. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs and no server of ours. Your photos are processed in memory on your iPhone and are never uploaded.

1. What we do not collect

We do not collect, receive, store, sell, rent, share or broker any of the following, because the app has no way to obtain them:

  • Your photos or videos. The app requests add-only Photo Library permission: it can save masked results, and it cannot read, alter or delete what you already have.
  • Face data or biometric templates. Face detection runs on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Detection results exist only in memory while a photo is being processed and are discarded when you leave the screen.
  • Identity data, contact details, location, browsing history, usage data, diagnostics, or an advertising identifier.
  • Any device identifier linked to you. There is no server for it to be sent to.

Accordingly, in the App Store privacy label this app is “Data Not Collected”, and that label is a factual description rather than a policy choice.

2. What stays on your device

The app keeps a minimal amount of state in its own sandboxed storage on your iPhone, none of which can leave the device:

Deleting the app removes all of it. On iOS 15 and later you can also offload or reset the app's data from Settings without losing your photos.

3. The one connection the app makes

When you buy or restore Face Shield Pro, Apple's StoreKit framework talks to Apple's storefront to confirm the transaction. That exchange happens between your device and Apple; we never receive your name, email, payment details or order identifier. Apple's Privacy Policy governs that traffic. We learn only that a purchase exists on your device.

Outside of StoreKit the app performs no network requests. It does not request network-server permission, does not load remote content, and displays no remote images.

4. Where masked photos go

Masked results are written as new photos into your own Photo Library. The app strips GPS coordinates and other location metadata from those copies, so the images you post do not reveal where the original was taken. Originals are untouched. We never see either.

5. Children

Face Shield is a tool used by adults (parents, teachers, photographers, event organisers) to protect images of children. The app collects no data from anyone, children included, so no child data is ever processed by us. It is designed to reduce the public exposure of children's faces, not to profile them.

6. This website

This support and privacy site is hosted by the developer. It serves static files only: no cookies, no local storage, no analytics, no third-party scripts or fonts, and no access logs are retained. Your browser's own settings govern anything it sends.

7. Your rights

There is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete about you, because we hold nothing. If you want written confirmation of that, or you believe this policy is inaccurate, email liangsx.168@gmail.com and you will get a reply from a human — the developer.

8. Changes

If this policy ever changes in a way that affects you, this page will be updated and the date above will change. Because the app collects nothing, a change that increases collection would require a new app version, a new permission prompt and an updated App Store privacy label — it cannot happen quietly.
