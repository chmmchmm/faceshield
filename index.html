Common questions

I exported photos — where did they go?

Masked images are saved as new photos into your Photo Library (they appear at the top of Recents). Your original photos are never modified or deleted — the app holds add-only photo permission, so it cannot change or remove anything you already have. Location data (EXIF GPS) is stripped from the masked copies.

It says I've used my free exports. How do I get more?

The free version includes 3 exports. Unlocking Face Shield Pro removes the limit. Go to Pro in the app to see current prices, then tap Restore Purchases if you already bought Pro on this Apple Account.

I paid, but the app still shows the paywall.

Tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the paywall. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account that made them, so make sure the same account is signed in on this device. If it still fails, email us with your App Store receipt — that is enough for us to fix it.

Two masking styles?

Mosaic (pixelated tiles) and Solid (opaque block). Both are verified: the app re-scans its own output and will not let you export while a face can still be detected in a masked area. That check is the whole point — a mosaic a detector can still read is not a mask.

What are “Hide my kid” and “Keep my kid”?

  • Hide my kid — you are posting a group photo and want every child masked before it goes online.
  • Keep my kid — you want your own child to stay recognizable while everyone else is masked.

Both start from “mask every detected face”, because a missed face is the only mistake you cannot undo.

Does it work offline? What happens to my photos?

Yes. Face detection and masking run entirely on your iPhone. The app declares no network permission, so it cannot upload your photos even if you wanted it to. The only connection it ever makes is StoreKit talking to Apple when you buy or restore Pro.
